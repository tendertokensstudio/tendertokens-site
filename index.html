meet me

Hi, I’m the human behind Tender Tokens.

I’m a registered nurse, writer, artist, and endlessly curious maker.

I’ve always loved creating things—books, scrapbooks, hand lettering, little projects that help me make sense of the world.

And then AI entered my creative life.

What started as a tool for answering questions became something much more interesting: a collaborator that helps me explore ideas, organize ambitious projects, experiment with new skills, and actually finish things.

I don’t believe using AI makes my work any less personal. My experiences, creative direction, judgment, and decisions remain central to everything I make.

I’m learning how to combine human imagination with emerging technology, and Tender Tokens Studio is one place where that exploration comes to life.

Someday, I hope to help others discover what they can create with AI, too.

For now, I’m making things, learning as I go, and seeing where curiosity takes me.
